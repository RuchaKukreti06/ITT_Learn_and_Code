This assignment allowed me to go through various types of comments and see their practical usage:

- The impact of comments in the code in both the good and the bad ways  
- Look into the categories of comments I should allow in my code and the drawbacks of several types of comments.
- The necessity of comments.
- I learned that the code should be self-explanatory, and comments should be used rarely when really needed.
- differentiate between good and bad comments

For particular comments I learned:

1. TODO comments should clearly state what needs to be done.
2. Redundant and noisy comments should be avoided.
3. Journal comments or attributions and bylines are not required now, as they are taken care of by the version control system.
4. Good comments, like warning comments, are acceptable, as they are required for the developers.
